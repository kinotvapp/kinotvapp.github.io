What Kino is

Everything you see in Kino arrives through plugins: small pieces of code, published by anyone, that connect a content source to the app. They're the engine behind everything else — not an add-on, but how Kino brings content in.

Kino is a movie and series player for phone and TV (Android TV / Fire TV): it adapts itself to a big screen with a remote or a phone screen with your fingers.

No ads, no trackers reporting what you watch, and it installs nothing besides the app itself.

How it was born

Kino started as a focused rebuild of a larger project: the idea was to do one thing well —play video, on any screen— instead of carrying everything that project had accumulated. What was left is a light player, built to work without depending on a server of its own: everything runs inside the app.

Plugins

A plugin is a content source anyone can publish as a small public GitHub repository. You install one by typing owner/repo, see which sites it will connect to, and from then on it's one more source: its results show up in search and on Home alongside everything else.
